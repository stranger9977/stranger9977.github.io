## Where the gap is

Both **buzz** numerators reproduce exactly (44 pooled, 18 in the vet stratum). That means the
outcome definition (total-PPR positional finish QB12/RB24/WR36/TE12) and the exposure mapping are
almost certainly right. The entire discrepancy sits in the **silent** group: ~12 player-seasons
are startable-and-silent in this reconstruction that were not in the article's.

Diagnosed cause: the projected-starter screen. The spec's literal minimums (startable finish
6-game min, startable PPG 5-game min, 60%+ snaps across 8+ games) do not catch starters whose
**prior season was injury-shortened**. Concrete leakage in this reconstruction:

- Odell Beckham 2018 — prior year 4 games, 18.5 PPG, 76% snaps
- Christian McCaffrey 2025 — prior year 4 games, 12.0 PPG, 72% snaps
- Patrick Mahomes 2018 — prior year 1 game, 10.4 PPG, 94% snaps
- David Johnson 2018 — prior year 1 game, 13.0 PPG, 61% snaps

Each clears every "projected starter" intuition but falls under all three game minimums, so each
survives into the silent pool and then finishes startable. That is exactly the +12.

A grid over the minimums (see `scratchpad/nfl/reconcile.R`) shows relaxing the PPG minimum from
5 games to 3 and the snap minimum from 8 games to 6 lands pool = 6359 and silent n = 6094 exactly,
with silent hits 84 (vs 78). That is closer but was **not** adopted here — fitting four published
numbers by tuning three thresholds is overfitting, and the spec states the minimums explicitly.
The literal spec definition is what produced the table.

**To close this properly:** confirm whether the article's starter screen had an injury/short-season
clause (e.g. prior-year PPG above the positional startable line at ANY game count, or a
games-missed exception). That one rule most likely accounts for the whole gap.

## Cohort construction actually used

- Cohort: nflverse season rosters, QB/RB/WR/TE, 2018–2025, deduped to one row per player-season → 7841
- Projected starter (excluded, 1429): round-1 rookie | startable finish prior yr (≥6 g) |
  startable PPG prior yr (≥5 g) | ≥60% avg offensive snap share over ≥8 games prior yr
- Analysis pool: 6412
- Exposure: majority positive direction per player-season from `mentions.csv` (26 mixed ties
  dropped; negative-majority treated as unexposed). 530 non-tie player-seasons, 528 matched to
  gsis_id after alias fixes (Ben→Benjamin Watson, Chris→Christopher Herndon, Scotty→Scott Miller,
  Joshua→Josh Palmer, accent folding for Estimé). Unmatched: "Roc Taylor" 2025 — never appears on
  an nflverse roster (UDFA who did not make a 53), correctly outside the cohort.
- Strata: rookie (1923) / vet prior-yr PPG < 8 (4111) / vet prior-yr PPG ≥ 8 (378)
- MH pooled = Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio, Σ(aᵢn0ᵢ/nᵢ) / Σ(bᵢn1ᵢ/nᵢ), rookie and vet≥8 strata
  held fixed while the floor moves the vet<8 stratum only. Floors applied symmetrically to both
  exposure groups, as required.

## Where the lift stabilizes

The decay is monotonic and front-loaded. Nearly half the raw stratum lift disappears at the very
first floor: requiring only that a player logged one game with any PPR production the prior year
drops the vet<8 lift from 12.8x to 8.1x, because it removes 1,779 silent player-seasons (45%) who
carried almost no chance of a startable finish while removing only 3 of 18 buzz hits. Past that
point the ladder flattens — 6.9x, 6.1x, then 4.7x at the most aggressive PPG≥4 floor — and the
Mantel-Haenszel pooled lift, which is the number that survives stratum-mix effects, is markedly
more stable still: 9.9x → 8.4x → 7.8x → 7.4x → 6.9x. The week-1-53-man floor, which is the most
defensible "had a realistic path" screen because it is a roster fact rather than a production
threshold, lands at 6.0x stratum / 7.3x pooled — squarely inside that band. So Nick's concern is
real in direction but bounded in size: the silent pool is genuinely padded, and the honest pooled
effect is roughly 7x rather than the ~10–13x the unfiltered comparison implies, with the estimate
stabilizing between about 6.9x and 7.8x across every floor that requires any real prior-year
involvement. The caveat above applies — absolute values shift once the starter-screen gap is closed.
