Sports analytics has always been my side quest.

Back in my middle-school-science-teaching days, this was the thing I did on the side. Now I’m a data scientist at a major sportsbook, and it’s still the thing I do on the side: the research projects that start in group chats, in sports debates with boomers, in The Conversation as a whole.

This site is where those quests get written up properly, with the data attached and the charts you can poke at.
